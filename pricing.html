Pricing FAQ

Billing & Booking Questions

Clear answers on deposits, refunds, and what's included.

What's included in the base price?

Every package includes venue hire, furniture, linens, staff, setup, and cleanup. There are no hidden service fees — the price you see is what you pay (plus tax).

How does the deposit work?

A 25% deposit secures your date. The remaining 75% is due 14 days before your event. We accept bank transfer, card, and instalment plans on request.

What qualifies for seasonal pricing?

Seasonal pricing (15% off) applies to weekday events between November and March (excluding December 20–January 5). Weekend events in these months pay standard rates.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. You can upgrade your package up to 30 days before your event. Simply pay the difference at the time of upgrade.

What's your cancellation policy?

Full refund if cancelled more than 60 days before your event. 50% refund between 30–60 days. Non-refundable within 30 days. All cancellations must be in writing.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — we offer 3- and 6-month instalment plans on all packages above $4,000. Ask your planner for details.

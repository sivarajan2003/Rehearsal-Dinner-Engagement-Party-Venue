Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before booking.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 4–6 months in advance for weekend dates and 2–3 months for weekday events. Peak season (May–September) fills quickly.

Can we bring our own vendors?

Yes — for photography, music, and cake, you're welcome to bring your own vendors (a small coordination fee applies). Catering is exclusively in-house.

What is the deposit and cancellation policy?

A 25% deposit secures your date. Full refund if cancelled more than 60 days before the event. 50% refund between 30–60 days. Non-refundable within 30 days.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Our chef designs menus around vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and allergy-specific requirements at no additional cost.

Is there parking and accessibility?

Yes. All venues have on-site parking and are fully wheelchair accessible. Shuttle service can be added for guests staying at nearby hotels.

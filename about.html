Who We Are

A Team Obsessed
With Your Moment.

Vowora began as a single garden terrace and a simple idea: that intimate celebrations deserve the same care, craft, and attention as grand weddings. A decade later, we've grown into six distinct venues — each with its own character, all sharing the same soul.

We're planners, chefs, florists, and perfectionists. We sweat the small stuff — the linen fold, the candle height, the first song — so you don't have to.

Whether it's 20 guests at a candlelit cellar table or 120 on a rooftop under the stars, we bring the same obsession to every celebration.

Our Journey

A Decade of Celebrations

From one garden terrace to six unique venues — here's how we grew. Scroll horizontally to explore.

2014

The Garden Pavilion Opens

Our first venue — a simple garden terrace — hosts its first rehearsal dinner for 40 guests.

2017

Library Lounge Opens

An intimate indoor space with vintage charm, velvet seating, and a fireplace — perfect for small dinners.

2018

Rooftop Terrace Debuts

Panoramic city views and a chic modern bar — our largest space opens for grand engagement parties.

2021

Client Dashboard Launch

We launch our custom RSVP dashboard — giving couples real-time control over guest lists and menus.

2023

Cellar & Lakeside Open

Two new intimate venues — a candlelit stone cellar and a sunset-facing lakeside deck.

Today

Six Venues, 280+ Celebrations

Vowora is now one of the region's most-loved intimate event destinations.

What We're Actually
Doing on the Day.

While you're getting ready and enjoying your guests, our team is running a tightly choreographed operation behind the scenes. Here's what's happening:

8:00 AM — Venue setup begins

Florals installed, tables set, lighting checked, bar stocked.

2 hours before — Vendor arrivals

Photographer, musicians, and any external vendors are greeted and briefed.

1 hour before — Kitchen final checks

Head chef confirms courses, dietary notes, and service timing with the floor team.

During the event — Dedicated floor lead

Your planner stays on-site, coordinating every moment to your run-sheet.

After the last guest — Full cleanup

Every glass, napkin, and candle is packed away. You leave with nothing to do.

Process FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you might want to know before step one.

How long does the whole process take?

From discovery call to event day, most couples book 4–6 months ahead. We can compress to 6 weeks for last-minute celebrations — ask us.

Will I have the same planner throughout?

Yes. From your first call to the final toast, one dedicated planner owns your event. No hand-offs, no repeating yourself.

What happens if I need to change the date?

Date changes are free up to 90 days before your event, subject to availability. Within 90 days, a £200 admin fee applies.

Can I skip steps if I'm short on time?

Absolutely. The tour and proposal can be combined into a single visit if you're travelling from out of town. Talk to your planner.

Do I need to be involved in every detail?

Not at all. You can choose how hands-on to be — from "I want to approve everything" to "just show me on the day." Your call.

What if I have specific cultural or religious requirements?

We've hosted celebrations across many traditions and we're happy to accommodate. Tell us in the discovery call and we'll design around your needs.
